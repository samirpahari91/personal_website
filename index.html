    Training data

    Three institutions, two continents. My geomatics engineering degree built the measurement foundation: surveying, GNSS, photogrammetry. The geoscience master's added process and hazards. The PhD is where the two meet machine learning at the scale of whole satellite archives.

    layer 02 · research head

    What I'm working on

    One question runs through all of it: where will the water go, and who is in the way? The methods range from a 1990s empirical soil-loss equation to patch-based convolutional networks, but the inputs are always real terrain: LiDAR, Sentinel-2, soils, rainfall.

    Publications

    Open source

    geoai-datacubes (BuckAI Observatory, MIT license) retrieves observations from 39 satellite missions through one interface and harmonizes them into aligned multi-mission datacubes for visualization and baseline ML. Paired with an AI coding assistant, a researcher can describe a study area and a time window in plain language and get the datacube back. I presented it at the Google | Ohio State partnership announcement in October 2026.
